# ChingYing Addons — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

This policy explains what data the **ChingYing Addons** Discord bot and the services behind the ChingYingAddons Minecraft mod ("the Service") use, why, and what you can do about it. The Service is run by the developer of ChingYingAddons, based in the Netherlands.

## 1. What we store

We only keep what a feature needs to work:

| Data | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Your Discord user ID and the Minecraft name you register | So chat-bridge messages from Discord show under your Minecraft name | Until you ask us to remove it or register a different name |
| Minecraft name, the cosmetics granted to it, when and by which moderator | So the mod can show your cosmetics | Until the cosmetic is removed or you ask us to delete it |
| Your Discord user ID and your vote on a suggestion | One vote per person, and a vote you can change | As long as the suggestion exists |
| Discord user ID, reason and end time of a mute | So a temporary mute is lifted on time, even after a restart | Until the mute ends |
| Discord user ID of whoever opened a bug ticket, and the ticket channel | So the ticket can be closed properly | Until the ticket is closed |

## 2. What we process but don't store

- **Chat bridge messages** are passed between the Discord channel and players in-game as they happen. The Service does not save them; they stay in Discord like any other message in that channel.
- **Dungeon sync and the map viewer** share room and party data between players in the same run, using a short temporary pairing code. This lives in memory only and disappears when the run ends.
- **Calculator commands** send the Minecraft name you ask about to a public Hypixel SkyBlock statistics service to look up levels. We don't keep the result.
- **Server logs** record technical events (for example "muted user X", errors, failed requests). They are used only to fix problems and prevent abuse, and are visible only to the developer and moderators.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the services needed to run the bot (Discord, our hosting provider, and the statistics service above).

## 3. Where it's stored

The Service runs on a cloud hosting provider. Data is stored on that provider's servers and protected with access controls; only the developer can reach it.

## 4. Legal basis

We process this data because it's needed to provide the features you choose to use (performance of a service), and for moderation and abuse prevention (legitimate interest).

## 5. Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to its use. Open a ticket with the **Submit a bug** button in the ChingYing Addons Discord server and say what you'd like. We'll respond within 30 days. If you live in the EU, you can also complain to your data protection authority (in the Netherlands: the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

## 6. Children

The Service is meant for people old enough to use Discord. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## 7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.

## 8. Contact

Open a ticket with the **Submit a bug** button in the ChingYing Addons Discord server.
